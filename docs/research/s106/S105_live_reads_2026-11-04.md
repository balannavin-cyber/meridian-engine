## 5. `hist_spot_bars_1m` grain and coverage (OFL aside, read 16:30–21:20 IST) — by design, no defect

Raised by the OFL stream: the first row for 2026-11-02 read `bar_ts 09:59` with open 75,408.69 → close 75,692.78, and ~361 of 375 bars. Measured with read-only SQL as `trade_date = 2026-11-02`, then `>= 2026-10-01`, and a read of `capture_cas_close.py` on the production box.

1. **The hole is the closing auction, every day.** Since at least 2026-10-01 every session has one gap, from 09:43/09:44 UTC (15:13/15:14 IST) to 09:59 UTC (15:29 IST). Cause: `capture_spot_1m_v2.py` stops at 15:15 IST (`MARKET_CLOSE_GUARD`, S70, ADR-022: the index is frozen through the auction). The 15:13 vs 15:14 jitter is probably the guard plus fetch lag (inferred, not read). Bars per day: 360–361, not 375.
2. **The 15:29 row is the CAS settled close, not a one-minute bar.** `capture_cas_close.py` (cron 16:20 IST) writes the vendor's OHLC for the 15:29 slot verbatim (lines 242–245, 472–475) to the canonical `CAS_CLOSE_BAR` slot (15, 29); `raw` carries `frozen_open`, `settled_close`, `settled_move` and the true vendor slot `bar_slot_ist` (the slot is {15:29, 15:34} by vendor stamping, ADR-022 A1.3). So **open = the frozen pre-auction index level, close = the auction's settled close**, and that bar is the widest of every day (11-02: SENSEX 284.09, NIFTY 109.70). Reading it as a one-minute range, or as a close spike, is wrong.
3. **Two `bar_ts` conventions in one table, boundary at 2026-04.** Apr 2025 – Mar 2026 (vendor era): IST labelled as `+00:00`, `:59` seconds, 09:00:59 → 16:00:59. From Apr 2026 (live writer): true UTC, `:00` seconds, 03:45 → 09:59. **RULE 16 ("IST labelled as UTC") holds for the vendor era only.** One stray vendor day in Nov 2025 reads 04:12:59 / 16:04:59 (the other convention).
4. **Withdrawn during this read:** a writer-defect reading ("last fetch missing", draft TD-S105-NEW-1) and a "coverage decaying" reading. Both were mine and both were wrong; the monthly bar-count differences are the same auction hole plus holidays.
5. **Open, small:** October 2026 has 20 trading days in the table but only 19 days with a hole row (10-20 and 10-21 are both absent from the hole list) — one day looks short or odd. Not read.

**For R(T) / the tape stream:** `hist_spot_bars_1m` is not a 375-row grid; the 15:14–15:29 window needs an explicit rule (exclude the 15:29 row, or carry it as a separate settlement observation), and any read across the 2026-04 boundary must normalise `bar_ts` per era.

The query (saved in the explorer as `rest anon readers by ip`; reuse for TD-S81-NEW-3):

```sql
select
  log_attributes['request.sb.jwt.authorization.payload.role'] as role,
  log_attributes['request.headers.cf_connecting_ip']           as ip,
  log_attributes['request.cf.country']                         as country,
  log_attributes['request.cf.asOrganization']                  as org,
  log_attributes['request.headers.user_agent']                 as ua,
  count() as hits, min(timestamp) as first_seen, max(timestamp) as last_seen
from logs
where source = 'edge_logs' and log_attributes['request.path'] like '/rest/v1/%'
group by role, ip, country, org, ua
order by hits desc limit 100
```

Note for the standing check: the template "Metadata IP" filters on `request.headers.x_real_ip`, a key this gateway does not write (it is absent from the key list); the IP is `request.headers.cf_connecting_ip`.
