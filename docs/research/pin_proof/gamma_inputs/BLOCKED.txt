FUTURES CONTRACT RULE (fixed 2026-11-20, BEFORE any sensitivity run):
  Recorded here before a single Fyers price is used in any computation, so the selection
  cannot be adjusted after seeing a result.
    F -- the close of the Fyers bar at the EXACT true-UTC instant: the sampled IST minute
      minus 5h30m, at offset 0. NO neighbour fill: a bar at +/-60 s is not substituted.
    CONTRACT -- among the contracts holding a bar at that instant, the one with the
      smallest expiry_date greater than or equal to the IST trade date. Chosen by
      measured expiry_date, never by contract_series, which is degenerate for SENSEX.
      The contract used at each minute is recorded. A minute where the contract used is
      not the one with the smallest expiry_date greater than or equal to the IST trade
      date among that day's contracts (because the nearer contract has no bar at that
      instant) is counted and reported as 'next contract used'; it is not excluded.
    MISSING F -- if no bar exists at that instant, F is missing for that minute; the
      minute is EXCLUDED and COUNTED. An excluded minute is reported, never silently
      dropped and never filled.
    T_fut = (expiry_date - IST trade date) / 365, in calendar days. This is the FUTURES'
      own time to expiry and never the option's T_opt (see the SCALE NOTE above).
    r = ln(F / S) / T_fut, with S the hist_spot_bars_1m close at the SAME IST-labelled
      bar_ts the option solve used, so that r is the only quantity changed.
    UNSTABLE -- a minute whose fut_dte is under 3 is flagged unstable, EXCLUDED and
      COUNTED, because a small T_fut denominator makes r explode.
    INTERPRETATION OF r -- it is a NET CARRY figure with dividends inside it
      (core/bs_engine.py:77-84 states this: for an index future the basis-implied rate is
      r - q, not r). It is not a risk-free rate and must not be described as one.
    VOLUME -- the bar's volume is reported alongside F, and a zero-volume bar is FLAGGED,
      because a stale print carries a stale basis.
    ALIGNMENT BASIS -- this rule adopts offset 0 under the true-UTC reading on the
      strength of the alignment test above, which covered two days only. It must be
      re-checked on a later pair (NIFTY 2026-03-04 and SENSEX 2026-03-02) after the
      24-day loop, and the rule is void if that re-check does not reproduce exact matches.
    SCOPE -- this rule covers F, T_fut, r and the exclusions only. The replication gate,
      the set of days and the pass criterion are not yet written and must be fixed in
      writing before any sensitivity run.
