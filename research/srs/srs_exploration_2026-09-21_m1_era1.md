## Method notes that change how these numbers read

1. **Price is `close`, not mid.** `hist_option_bars_1m` carries OHLC and no `bid`/`ask`, so the brief's mid-price does not exist in era 1. `close` is a last-trade print, which CLAUDE.md's settled `ltp` rule says is not an executable price. Era 2 carries `bid`/`ask`; the mid-vs-close divergence measured there is the error bar on every number below, and until that is measured these figures bracket the harvest point rather than choose it.
2. **Stamps are clock-anchored.** Last observation at or before 15:25 IST (10:30 for the Day-1 reference, 13:00 on expiry day), rejected if older than 5 minutes. `max(bar_ts)` is NOT the close -- the spot relations run to 16:00 IST post-market and open at 09:07 pre-market.
3. **`zpv`** is the share of stamps whose PRECEDING 1-minute bar traded zero volume. It is a staleness indicator, not a decay figure. A high `zpv` means the decay in that row rests on prints that may be minutes old, which inflates apparent stability.
4. **Cycles come from the chain's own `expiry_date`.** Two cheaper sources were refuted: `hist_atm_option_bars_5m` mixes weekly and monthly series, and `volatility_snapshots` rolls early -- wrong on expiry days specifically, which are the only days that set a cycle boundary.
