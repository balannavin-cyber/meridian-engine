## What this cannot say

- **Nothing after 2026-03-30.** The pull stops there because option bars stop there (lookback_v4 Part 0a). No statement here extends to the live board or to any date in the last eight months of the v4 window.
- **OI is not dealer inventory.** `oi_call` / `oi_put` are total open interest. Who is long or short, and therefore which way anyone must hedge, is not in this data. 'Amplifying' in M5 is a label for oi_put > oi_call, not a measured dealer position.
- **An OTM close can be an old print.** `px` is the 1-minute bar close; an illiquid far strike may not have traded in that minute, or that hour. Its sigma_K is then solved from a stale price, and the fallback count only catches the prices that are zero or unsolvable, not the stale ones.
- **The walls here use the study's sigma, not the vendor's.** `sigma_wall = S·sigma_atm·sqrt(max(dte_cal,1)/252)` reconstructs the Board's rule with sigma_atm solved from the straddle, where the live view uses `volatility_snapshots.atm_iv_avg`. The band shape is the same; the number is not the Board's. M2 is therefore about the rule, not about what the Board drew on any given day.
- Pins are computed on bars at 24 fixed minutes, not continuously, so M3 change counts and M4 windows are a 15-minute sampling of the path.
